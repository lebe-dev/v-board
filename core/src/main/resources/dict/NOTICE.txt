Bundled dictionaries (en.tsv, ru.tsv)

Derived from wordfreq by Robyn Speer
https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq
It aggregates many open corpora (OpenSubtitles, Wikipedia, subtitles, Twitter,
and others) into a single de-duplicated frequency per word.

Package code: Apache License 2.0
https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0
Frequency data: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
See https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq/blob/master/docs/data.rst for the full
list of sources and their licenses.

Changes: lowercased, filtered to words of 2+ letters of the target alphabet,
duplicates merged, truncated to the most frequent entries; common Russian
words with a hyphen added, with a frequency estimated by wordfreq from their
parts and divided by 10 per hyphen (see scripts/fetch_dictionaries.py).

Word pair table (ru.bigrams)

Counted from Russian Wikipedia, as published in the wikimedia/wikipedia dataset
(configuration 20231101.ru): https://huggingface.co/datasets/wikimedia/wikipedia
Text: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0),
by the contributors of Russian Wikipedia: https://ru.wikipedia.org/
The table holds only counts of adjacent word pairs, not text; it is built by
scripts/swipe_model/bigrams.py (see `just bigrams`), and is shared under the
same licence.
