Emoji catalog and pictures
==========================

emoji.tsv is generated by scripts/fetch_emoji.py from emoji-datasource-apple 16.0.0
(https://github.com/iamcal/emoji-data), MIT License, Copyright (c) 2013 Cal Henderson.

The pictures in img/ come from the same package. They are Apple's artwork, are not stored in
the repository and are downloaded at build time.
